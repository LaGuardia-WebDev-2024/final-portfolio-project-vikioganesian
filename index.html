Helloo, I'm Viki Insomnia, a visual artist based in New York City. I view my work as a visual and physical manifestation of who I am and who I aspire to become. As I continue to grow as an artist, I hope to expand my art beyond paintings by exploring filmmaking, and clothing design. I am interested in how different creative aspects can connect with one another and create new way of expressing my ideas.
